Good to know before you buy

Please inspect and test items before leaving. For purchases over $5, report defects or missing parts within 24 hours for an exchange or store credit. Cash refunds and change-of-mind returns are not accepted. Clothing and shoes are final sale.

The weekly rhythm

Fresh finds Saturday. Prices drop through Friday.

New stock arrives Saturday. Prices then decrease based on remaining inventory. Friday prices are often around $5, with some items as low as $1.
